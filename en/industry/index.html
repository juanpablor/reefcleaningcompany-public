    • Data Centers

    RFM is a high-performance, eco-conscious floor cleaner expertly designed to meet the unique requirements of data centers.  Its advanced formula enhances the operation of cleaning floors while ensuring the integrity and safety of high-tech environments.  A solution that was tested along six months in several data centers by one of the most important energy companies in the US and has proven its formulation.

    Optimized for Data Center Environments - RFM:

  • Anti-Static Properties: RFM's specialized formulation reduces static electricity, a critical feature for protecting sensitive electronic equipment in data centers.
  • Low Residue Impact: ensures that floors remain clean without leaving behind residues that could attract dust, critical for maintaining server efficiency and cooling systems.
  • Chemical Safety: the non-corrosive, neutral pH range (7.5 - 8.5) of RFM ensures it is safe for use around sophisticated data center machinery, preventing any potential damage or clogging of internal systems.
  • Gentle Yet Effective on Various Floors: RFM is a gentle yet effective cleaning solution designed for the diverse flooring types found in data centers, including anti-static mats, raised floor tiles, and sealed surfaces. It maintains the integrity and appearance of these surfaces without the use of harsh chemicals. Additionally, RFM is highly dilutable and cost-effective, offering versatile cleaning options that cater to different levels of soiling and specific facility needs, ensuring both quality and efficiency without compromising on performance.
  • Eco-Friendly and Safe: with a 100% biodegradable and non-toxic formula, RFM aligns with sustainability goals while ensuring the safety of both workers and the delicate data center environment.
  • Enhanced Safety Features: the anti-slip properties of RFM make it ideal for maintaining safe floor conditions in busy data center environments, where safety and uptime are paramount.
  • Customizable Dilution: the dilution ratio of RFM can be tailored based on the specific cleaning requirements of the data center, dictated by the tank size of the cleaning machine and the level of floor dirtiness.
